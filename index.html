About Me

I’m Abdelrahman Sabry, a Professional Video Editor and Video Strategist with over 3 years of experience. I specialize in transforming raw footage into high-retention visual assets that drive engagement and sales. With a keen eye for rhythm, storytelling, and advanced proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects, I help brands and creators dominate their niche through cinematic editing, motion graphics, and strategic content. Let’s build your visual legacy together.

Career Objective

To leverage my 3+ years of professional editing experience to forge strategic partnerships with top-tier creators and global brands, delivering cinematic visual stories that define industry standards.
